Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Overview

NumChecker ("we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy of our users and customers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website, purchase our software, or use our standalone desktop application.

2. Local Desktop Data Processing (Zero Cloud Data Storage)

Your contact lists and leads remain 100% private on your own computer:

  • NumChecker is a standalone Windows desktop software. All phone number validations, formatting, and CRM spreadsheet processing happen locally on your PC.
  • We do not upload, store, harvest, or transmit your contact spreadsheets, customer phone numbers, or lead data to our servers.
  • Your WhatsApp Web login session is stored locally in your own computer's application data folder.

3. Information We Collect When Purchasing

When you purchase NumChecker through authorized payment processors (WarriorPlus, Gumroad, or AppSumo), our payment partners collect the necessary transactional details:

  • Name, email address, and billing information (processed securely via PayPal, Stripe, or payment merchant accounts).
  • We never have access to or store your credit card details on our servers.

4. How We Use Your Purchase Information

We use your purchase details solely to:

  • Fulfill your digital software license and download links.
  • Provide technical customer support and software update notices.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and process valid refunds.

5. Third-Party Services & Links

Our website utilizes standard affiliate and analytics tracking scripts (such as WarriorPlus tracking) to attribute commissions to authorized promoters. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
